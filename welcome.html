Connect Your Calendar

Video placeholderStep 3 video: Google Calendar, booking calendar and Zoom (to be recorded)

Meetings on Zoom? Link Zoom first.

Before you start: Settings, then My Profile, then Video Conferencing, then Add new. Sign in and connect. You’ll pick Zoom under Staff and Location in Part B.

A. Link Google Calendar

  1. Click Integrations.
  2. Click Google Calendar.
  3. Sign in and connect.

B. Set up your booking calendar

  1. Go to Settings, then Calendars, then Meetings.
  2. Edit the first calendar (rename it if you like).
  3. Staff and Location: switch to Zoom (optional).
  4. Availability: add a staff member and set your hours.
  5. Click the three dots, then Activate Calendar.

Build Your Agent

Video placeholderStep 4 video: creating the agent, knowledge base, brain and booking (to be recorded)

Part 1: Create your agent and its knowledge

Create the agent

  1. Go to AI Agents, then Conversation AI.
  2. Click Create agent.
  3. Choose Prompt-based agent.
  4. Choose Appointment booking.

Add the knowledge base

  1. Click Knowledge Base.
  2. Click Create knowledge base.
  3. Paste Part 2.1 into Rich Text.
  4. Paste Part 2.3 into Rich Text.
  5. Add each FAQ and its answer.

Your FAQ answers are not written for you. Add your own, one at a time.

Part 2: Give your agent its brain

Paste each part here

  • Part 1.1 into Personality
  • Part 1.2 into Goal
  • Part 1.3 into Instructions. Remove the examples first.

Connect booking

  1. Click Set up your actions.
  2. Choose Appointment booking.
  3. Choose Single calendar.
  4. Pick the calendar you set up in Step 3.
  5. Save.
